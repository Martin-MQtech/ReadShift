其实，连当年中国的“文革”浩劫，也是由一群年轻人以“破旧立新”的口号开始的，而实际上却是一个彻底颠倒新旧的悲剧。这种情形，在传统厚重而又争斗成性的族群中更容易发生。

Even the Cultural Revolution—that cataclysmic upheaval in China—was launched by young people under the banner of “destroying the old to forge the new,” yet it became, in truth, a tragedy that inverted those very categories. Such inversions are most likely to emerge in societies carrying a heavy inheritance of tradition and a long-bred instinct for strife.

---

张忠谋先生的可贵，在于他以最隆重、最谨慎的方式完成了一种文化转型，成了一个现代创造者。因此早早地就浑身轻松。

Morris Chang’s distinction lies in the deliberation and near-ceremonial care with which he completed a cultural transformation—emerging not as a rebel against his inheritance, but as a modern creator who had metabolized it. And in doing so, he shed the weight early, moving through the world with an unusual lightness.

---

His autobiography declares, from the very first page, the two cultural currents that shaped him.
