## 一、山城里的求学梦

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">A Dream of Learning in the Mountain City</p>
</div>

当时的南开中学由著名教育家张伯苓创办，被誉为“难民学校中的精英摇篮”。在战火纷飞的年代，南开以极严格的学术标准和自律精神著称，录取率不足十分之一。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Founded by the distinguished educator Zhang Boling, Nankai Middle School was hailed as a cradle of excellence amid wartime disruption. Renowned for its rigorous academic standards and strict discipline, the school accepted less than ten percent of applicants.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
1. **Wartime sanctuary** — 战时避风港；指在动荡与危机中为精英培养与知识延续提供庇护的机构或环境。
**商业造句**：During economic downturns, resilient corporate academies function as a wartime sanctuary for top talent.

2. **Rigorous selection** — 严格筛选机制；指极低的录取率或门槛，用以筛选具备极强抗压能力与自律精神的后备力量。
**商业造句**：The firm's rigorous selection process accepts less than five percent of applicants worldwide.
